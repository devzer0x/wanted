# How this world works — the rules that keep you unstuck

**Wanted stars (1–5).** 1: they try to arrest you. 2: they shoot. 3: roadblocks, spike strips, a
helicopter. 4–5: armoured units, several helicopters — survival, not a fight. **Losing them:**
get out of every officer's line of sight and stay hidden until the flashing stars stop (roughly
30 s at one star up to ~90 s at five); being seen resets it. Changing car while unseen helps
(they track your last vehicle). Driving into **Los Santos Customs** clears the stars if nobody
saw you enter; reaching a **safehouse** clears them below three stars.

**Wasted.** You wake up outside the nearest hospital, lose a little cash, keep your weapons.
Mid-mission, the game offers **Retry from the last checkpoint** — take it. **Busted** (arrest when
you are nearly dead and a cop closes in): police station, ammo and armour gone.

**Mission failed → Retry.** Retry resumes from the last checkpoint and is only offered right
after the failure; decline it and you have to go back to the start marker. After three failures
at the same point the game adds **Skip** — never take it; a skipped mission is a lost one.
Missions have checkpoints: dying costs you the segment, not the job.

**Health and armour.** Health regenerates only up to half the bar, and only while you stand still
— cover speeds it up. Above half you need food or a drink. **Armour** (the blue bar) soaks damage
first, never regenerates, and is bought at Ammu-Nation. Sprinting to exhaustion hurts you.

**Cover** snaps you to the nearest wall or car — use it when red dots are close. **Special
abilities**: Michael slows time in a fight, Franklin slows time while driving, Trevor takes and
deals more damage; they recharge on their own.

**Starting a job**: walk or drive into the coloured marker at the contact's spot; a scene plays.
Jobs give you the vehicle they need; if they hand you one, keep it alive.
